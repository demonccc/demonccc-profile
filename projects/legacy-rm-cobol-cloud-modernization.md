## Overview & Motivation

A freelance modernization effort focused on keeping a legacy RM/COBOL business system operational after its original SCO Unix hardware and software environment became increasingly difficult to sustain.

## Architecture & Implementation

The solution replatformed the legacy environment onto modern infrastructure rather than rewriting the business application from scratch.

The documented implementation moved the workload toward AWS EC2 and used Linux-hosted compatibility and virtualization layers so the existing RM/COBOL software could continue operating while the surrounding hardware and operating environment were modernized.

A related local setup used Ubuntu with DOS/FreeDOS-compatible execution where required to preserve integration with fiscal-printing workflows and older device dependencies.

## My Contribution

I designed and implemented the migration approach, assembled the compatibility stack, configured remote access and solved the operating-system and hardware-integration problems required to keep the business application functional on newer infrastructure.

## Key Capabilities Demonstrated

Legacy modernization, pragmatic cloud migration, Linux systems engineering, compatibility-layer design and the ability to preserve business continuity when a full application rewrite is not economically or operationally justified.

## Results & Lessons

The project extended the useful life of a business-critical legacy application while reducing dependence on obsolete physical hardware.

It also reinforced a recurring engineering principle in my career: modernization does not always mean rewriting. The right solution depends on business risk, cost, time and the actual constraint being removed.

## Tech Stack

RM/COBOL (RM-85), SCO Unix, AWS EC2, Ubuntu Linux, VirtualBox, SSH and FreeDOS-compatible tooling.
