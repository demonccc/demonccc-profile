## Overview & Motivation

A long-running lab using Raspberry Pi systems as low-power multimedia and embedded computing nodes for audio, video, home-theater and emulation workloads.

## Architecture & Implementation

The lab includes specialized Linux distributions and services for networked audio, home cinema and emulation. Systems are integrated with local storage and network services rather than treated as isolated appliances.

Audio experiments include Volumio and Moode Audio with ALSA-based output toward external DACs. Video and home-theater work includes Kodi / LibreELEC and network-mounted media. Retro-computing experiments include RetroPie and Libretro-based environments.

## My Contribution

I selected and assembled the hardware, installed and tuned the operating systems, configured network storage and multimedia services, debugged Linux/audio/video issues and integrated the nodes into the broader home infrastructure.

## Key Capabilities Demonstrated

Linux systems integration, SBC platforms, networked multimedia, embedded troubleshooting and pragmatic optimization of constrained hardware.

## Results & Lessons

The lab provides stable, low-power multimedia nodes and practical experience with the interaction between Linux, hardware acceleration, networking, audio pipelines and real-world consumer devices.

## Tech Stack

Raspberry Pi, Linux, ALSA, Volumio, Moode Audio, Kodi, LibreELEC, RetroPie, Libretro and NFS.
