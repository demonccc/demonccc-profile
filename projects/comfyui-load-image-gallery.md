## Overview & Motivation

Built to reduce friction when selecting images and model resources in ComfyUI environments containing large collections of references, checkpoints and LoRAs.

## Architecture & Implementation

The extension replaces rigid selection workflows with a richer gallery-style interface integrated into the ComfyUI canvas, combining Python-side node behavior with frontend customization.
