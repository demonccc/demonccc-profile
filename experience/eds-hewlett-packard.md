# EDS / Hewlett-Packard

## Mission & Context

This was one continuous employment period. Hewlett-Packard acquired EDS while I was already working there. The company identity changed, but my role and operational responsibilities remained essentially the same.

The work focused on enterprise Unix infrastructure and global operations, including the Sabre environment.

## What I Actually Did

- Provided technical support for enterprise Unix environments including HP-UX, AIX, Solaris and Red Hat Linux.
- Diagnosed infrastructure problems, performed Root Cause Analysis and applied or recommended corrective actions.
- Managed technical requests, incidents and infrastructure changes under formal operational processes.
- Worked with distributed operations teams in the United States and India.
- Administered storage and clustering technologies including LVM, Veritas Volume Manager and Veritas Cluster Server.
- Worked with remote hardware-management interfaces such as HP iLO, Sun OpenBoot PROM and Dell DRAC.
- Supported infrastructure services such as DNS and LDAP.

## Tech Stack & Methods

HP-UX, AIX, Solaris, Red Hat Linux, LVM, Veritas Volume Manager, Veritas Cluster Server, DNS, LDAP, ITIL, incident management, change management and Root Cause Analysis.
