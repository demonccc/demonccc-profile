## Mission & Context

Returned to Globant in 2015 and grew into a Subject Matter Expert and CloudOps / DevOps leadership role within the CloudOps Studio, working across global clients and distributed engineering teams.

## What I Actually Did

- Led re-engineering and modernization initiatives for mission-critical systems in banking and airline environments.
- Designed cloud-native architectures and migration strategies across AWS, Azure and GCP.
- Led multicultural engineering teams distributed across North America, Europe, India and Latin America.
- Advised clients on DevSecOps, CI/CD, cloud architecture, observability and operating-model changes.
- Participated in technical pre-sales, architecture proposals and estimation for enterprise opportunities.
- Applied FinOps thinking to cloud migration and explained CAPEX-to-OPEX implications to stakeholders.
- Drove adoption of containers, Kubernetes and GitOps practices including ArgoCD and Spinnaker.
- Designed reusable Pipeline-as-Code approaches using Jenkins Groovy Shared Libraries.
- Mentored engineers and acted as a technical reference for complex architecture and operations problems.

## Representative Contexts

The documented work includes banking initiatives such as OpenBank / Santander España and Citibanamex, and airline-sector work including LATAM Airlines.
