## Mission & Context

Led and evolved engineering capabilities around DevOps, QA, Developer Productivity and platform integration in a regulated banking environment. The scope moved from hands-on DevOps leadership toward broader engineering management and, later, strategic integration architecture.

The role progression included Technical Leader / Head of DevOps, Manager / Head of DevOps and Digital Monitoring, Senior Manager / Head of DevOps and QA, and Senior Manager / Strategic Integration Architect.

## What I Actually Did

- Defined and transformed the Software Development Life Cycle framework, redesigning policies and processes around traceability, security, quality and operational efficiency.
- Led alignment of delivery processes with BCRA requirements, contributing to the Zero Audit Recommendations milestone and a fully auditable delivery model.
- Provided technical direction for an in-house deployment platform based on event-driven architecture and secure APIs.
- Led internal product work around DevOps, Quality, Monitoring, ETL and engineering metrics.
- Contributed to the functional design and adoption strategy of Santander's Global Internal Developer Portal.
- Led local integration of the Global IDP with local infrastructure and engineering workflows.
- Drove DORA, SPACE and Developer Experience measurement initiatives.
- Led Generative AI adoption for engineering and designed AI-agent concepts for SDLC automation.

## Key Initiatives

### Pipeline 2.0
- **Context:** Continuous delivery in a regulated environment required strong traceability, quality controls and explicit operational governance.
- **My contribution:** Technical direction of an Event-Driven Architecture solution and secured deployment APIs, including approval and freeze-window validation, end-to-end traceability and integration with the surrounding engineering ecosystem.
- **Architecture:** Go and Python services, MongoDB, Redis, GitLab CI, OpenShift, Nexus, SonarQube and the local IDP.
- **Outcome:** Organization-wide rollout of standardized deployment capabilities, including A/B, Blue-Green and automated rollback strategies, while supporting the audit and regulatory model.

### SDLC Governance & BCRA
- **My contribution:** Led process alignment, controls and auditable evidence generation across the SDLC.
- **Outcome:** Zero Audit Recommendations from external auditors and the BCRA during the documented period.

### Internal Developer Platforms
- **My contribution:** Led local IDP capabilities and later acted as a local Key Enabler for the Global IDP, coordinating integration with DevOps, QA and Security and participating in global technical leadership discussions.

### Engineering Data & Observability
- **My contribution:** Directed the implementation of ETL and engineering-data flows, DORA/SPACE dashboards and enterprise monitoring capabilities using Dynatrace, Prometheus, Grafana, Fluent Bit and PowerBI.

### Generative AI for Engineering
- **My contribution:** Led engineering-focused GenAI adoption and designed a PoC using AI Agents for GitHub and repository context/indexing to automate parts of the SDLC.

## Tech Stack & Methods

GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, GitHub Runners, Go, Python, MongoDB, Redis, OpenShift, AWS, Terraform, Pulumi, SonarQube, Nexus, Dynatrace, Prometheus, Grafana, Fluent Bit, PowerBI, DORA, SPACE, BCRA governance and Internal Developer Platforms.
